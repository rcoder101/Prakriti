Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026 · OllieWise LLC ("OllieWise", "we")

OllieWise is a private wellness companion. This policy covers the OllieWise app and olliewise.com. The short version: your answers stay yours, we do not sell data, we do not run ads, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

What the app collects

Wellness information you enter. OllieWise works by asking you about yourself. Your answers are what make the app work for you.

  • Onboarding answers: body characteristics, digestion and appetite, energy, sleep patterns, stress response, current symptoms, and daily life context (available time, daily rhythm, cooking access).
  • Ongoing checks: daily habit logs, a nightly mind check, and periodic sleep and wellness questionnaires.
  • What we derive from your answers: your constitutional profile, readings of your current state, and your personalized habit plan.

This is health related information. We never use it for advertising, never sell it, and never share it except with the service providers below, who process it only on our instructions.

Account information. The app creates an anonymous account automatically so your data can be restored if you reinstall or change devices. You can choose to link an email address or Apple ID to sign in on another device.

Technical information. Crash reports (device model, OS version, and the technical state of the app when it crashed) and app usage events (which features are used, including summary results of your assessments) tied to your anonymous account identifier. We use these only to fix problems and improve the app.

Approximate location, if you allow it. The app uses your approximate location to work out your climate zone and season. It keeps the climate zone, the hemisphere, and a latitude rounded to a whole degree. It never stores a precise location.

What we do NOT collect: your name (unless you email us), precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. We run no advertising trackers or pixels anywhere.

What the website collects

Waitlist email. If you join the waitlist on olliewise.com, we store the email address you submit, the time you submitted it, and whether you use an iPhone or an Android phone if you tell us. We use it to send a welcome note and to tell you when OllieWise opens. Giving it is voluntary.

Basic technical data. Our hosting provider processes standard connection information, such as IP address, to serve pages securely and prevent abuse. The website runs no analytics trackers and sets no cookies.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored on your device first, and the app works fully offline. It is also mirrored to our cloud database under your account identifier, so it can be restored. Service providers that process data for us:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, crash reporting, app analytics). Google processes this data on our behalf.
  • Cloudflare (website hosting), Resend (email delivery), and Google Fonts (typefaces). These serve the website only.
  • Apple TestFlight (during beta). If you send feedback or screenshots through TestFlight, Apple shares them with us under Apple's own terms.

Deletion, access, and your rights

  • Delete everything, in the app: Settings, then Delete account, removes your data from your device and from our cloud database. Settings, then Start over, erases your data and begins fresh.
  • A copy of your data: email hello@olliewise.com and we will send you what we hold, promptly.
  • We honor deletion and access rights for everyone, no matter where you live, including rights under California law and similar laws elsewhere. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Retention

Wellness data is kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete your account. Crash and usage records are retained on standard Firebase schedules and are not tied back to you after account deletion. Website waitlist emails are kept until launch invitations go out or you ask to be removed, whichever comes first.

Children

OllieWise is for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. Questions, deletions, copies of your data: hello@olliewise.com.
